Veylix – Privacy Monitor for Android

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Veylix – Privacy Monitor (“Veylix,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Information and device permissions

Veylix is designed to help users review privacy-related information available through Android, such as app permissions and supported privacy indicators. Available information depends on your Android version, device manufacturer, system restrictions, and permissions you grant.

The app may need access to Android permission or usage information to provide features you choose to use. Veylix does not itself record calls or capture microphone or camera content unless a clearly described feature explicitly does so.

2. How information is used

Information available to Veylix is used to provide privacy-monitoring and permission-review features, troubleshoot user-reported issues, and maintain app functionality.

Verify before publishing: If the released app processes information only on your device and sends nothing to a server, you may state that information is processed locally and is not received by the developer. Revise this statement if the app uses analytics, crash reporting, cloud APIs, or other data transmission.

3. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal information. Any third-party SDKs, analytics, crash-reporting tools, advertising services, or external APIs included in the released app must be disclosed here and in Google Play’s Data safety section as applicable.

4. Data retention and deletion

Information stored by the app, if any, may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. If the app sends information to a server or third-party service, describe retention periods and deletion methods here before publishing.

5. Permissions and your choices

You can review or change app permissions in Android Settings. Restricting permissions may limit related features. Android may show its own microphone, camera, or other privacy indicators; Veylix cannot bypass Android security controls and may not observe every activity by other apps.

6. Children’s privacy

Veylix is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer in connection with the app, contact us so we can review the request.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the app’s design to protect information under our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact starbuxtech@gmail.com.
